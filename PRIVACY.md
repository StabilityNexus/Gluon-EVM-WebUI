# Privacy Policy

## Introduction

Gluon-EVM WebUI (the App) is committed to protecting your privacy and providing a transparent and user-friendly experience.

This Privacy Policy explains how the App handles information when you use it.

The App follows a privacy-conscious and local-first approach.
It aims to collect and process only the information necessary to provide its functionalities.
Where possible, information is processed and stored locally on your device rather than being transmitted to or stored on remote servers.


## Information and Permissions

Depending on the features you use and the permissions you grant, the App may access information such as:

* Your public blockchain wallet address when you connect a wallet
* The blockchain network selected in your wallet or through the App
* Public blockchain information associated with addresses, tokens, reactors, and smart contracts
* Transaction information that you choose to prepare, sign, or submit through your wallet
* Information stored locally in your browser that is required for App functionality, such as whether you have accepted the Terms of Use for the current UTC day
* Wallet connection and session information handled by supported wallet connection libraries
* Technical request information that may be processed by third-party infrastructure used by the App, such as blockchain RPC providers, GitHub, wallet connection providers, and blockchain explorers

The App does not require access to your contacts, health information, precise location, photos, files, microphone, or camera for its normal functionality.

The App does not request or store your wallet password, recovery phrase, or private keys. Wallet signing and authorization are handled by your wallet.

The information accessed by the App is used only for the purposes described in this Privacy Policy and to provide the functionality of the App.

You can disconnect your wallet or revoke permissions through your wallet, browser, or device settings.
Some features may not work if the permissions or wallet connections required for those features are not available.


## Data Storage

The App is designed to minimize the storage and transmission of personal information.

The App itself does not operate a user-account database or a server for storing personal information.

Information required only for local App functionality may be stored in your browser. This may include:

* The UTC date on which you most recently accepted the Terms of Use
* Wallet connection or session information maintained by wallet connection libraries
* Other browser storage required for normal interface functionality

The App reads public blockchain data directly through blockchain infrastructure and supported wallet libraries.

Transactions that you choose to submit are recorded on the relevant public blockchain. Blockchain transactions and associated public addresses may be publicly visible and may be permanent and irreversible.

Third-party infrastructure used by the App may process or retain technical information according to its own privacy policies and terms of service.


## Data Sharing

The App does not sell your personal information.

The App does not use personal information for targeted advertising.

The App may communicate with third parties when required to provide specific features.
Any information transmitted to such services is limited to what is necessary for the App's functionality.

The following third parties or categories of third parties may receive information:

* **Blockchain networks and RPC providers:** Public wallet addresses, blockchain queries, transaction data, network information, and technical request metadata may be transmitted when the App reads blockchain data or when you submit transactions.
* **Wallet providers and WalletConnect-compatible services:** Information required to establish and maintain wallet connections may be processed when you connect a wallet. The App uses wallet connection libraries including RainbowKit and WalletConnect-compatible infrastructure.
* **GitHub:** GitHub infrastructure may be used to host the App or retrieve public project resources such as token lists and the Stability Nexus Terms of Use. GitHub may receive technical request information such as your IP address, browser information, and request details.
* **Blockchain explorers:** If you choose to open an external blockchain explorer link, your browser communicates directly with that explorer and the explorer may receive technical request information.

Public blockchain transactions are not private. Information included in a blockchain transaction may be visible permanently to anyone who accesses that blockchain.

Where a third party processes information, its handling of that information is governed by its own privacy policy and terms of service.


## Data Security

The App aims to minimize privacy and security risks by limiting unnecessary data collection and, where possible, processing information locally on your device.

The App does not request or store wallet private keys, recovery phrases, or wallet passwords.

Blockchain transactions must be authorized through your wallet.

However, no method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Users are responsible for maintaining the security of their devices, browsers, wallets, passwords, recovery phrases, and private keys.

Users are also responsible for reviewing transaction details before signing or submitting blockchain transactions.


## Data Deletion

Information stored locally by the App can generally be removed by clearing the App's site data, browser storage, or localStorage through your browser settings.

Wallet connection information may also be removed by disconnecting the wallet and clearing the relevant browser or wallet application data.

The App does not maintain a remote user database from which personal information must be deleted.

Information recorded on a public blockchain generally cannot be modified or deleted. Confirmed blockchain transactions and associated public data may remain permanently available on the blockchain.

Information processed or retained independently by third-party providers is subject to the deletion policies of those providers.


## Data Export

The App does not currently maintain user profiles or a centralized collection of personal information for export.

Public blockchain information associated with your wallet address can be independently accessed through blockchain nodes, wallets, blockchain explorers, and other compatible tools.

If you export, copy, download, or otherwise share information from your wallet, browser, blockchain explorer, or another service, you are responsible for protecting that information.


## Children's Privacy

The App is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children where such collection is prohibited by applicable law.

The App does not intentionally request age information or information specifically concerning children.

Users are responsible for ensuring that their use of the App complies with the laws applicable to them.


## Free Access

The App aims to keep its core functionality accessible to users without requiring mandatory subscriptions or payments to unlock essential features.

The App does not require a paid subscription to access its interface.

Blockchain interactions may require payment of blockchain network transaction fees, commonly referred to as gas fees.

Smart contracts accessed through the App may also apply protocol-level fees where such fees are defined by the applicable smart contract.

These blockchain or protocol fees are separate from access to the App itself.


## Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes to the App, its functionality, third-party infrastructure, or applicable legal requirements.

Any updates will be made available wherever this Privacy Policy is published.
